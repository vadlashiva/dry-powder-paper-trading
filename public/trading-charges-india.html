Trading charges in India explained: brokerage, STT, GST and stamp duty

Your contract note lists six different charges. Individually they look tiny; together they can swallow a large share of small intraday profits. Here's what each one is and how to estimate it.

Updated 31 October 2026

The six charges

ChargeWho sets itTypical amount
BrokerageYour brokerDiscount brokers: often ₹20 per executed order (or 0.03% intraday, whichever is lower); zero for delivery at many
STT (Securities Transaction Tax)GovernmentEquity delivery 0.1% buy & sell · equity intraday 0.025% sell · options 0.15% of premium on sell · futures 0.05% sell
Exchange transaction chargesNSE / BSEA small percentage of turnover — roughly 0.003% for equity and 0.035% of premium for options on NSE
SEBI turnover feeSEBI₹10 per crore of turnover
GSTGovernment18% on brokerage + exchange charges + SEBI fee
Stamp dutyGovernmentBuy side only: 0.015% delivery · 0.003% intraday and options

STT rates as revised from 1 April 2026 (options sale 0.15%, futures 0.05%). Exchange charges are approximate and revised from time to time.

Example 1: an intraday stock trade

Buy & sell ₹1,00,000 of shares the same dayApproximate charges
Brokerage (₹20 × 2 orders)₹40.00
STT (0.025% of ₹1,00,000 sell)₹25.00
Exchange charges (≈0.003% of ₹2,00,000)≈ ₹5.94
SEBI fee (₹10 per crore)₹0.20
Stamp duty (0.003% of ₹1,00,000 buy)₹3.00
GST (18% of brokerage + exchange + SEBI)≈ ₹8.31
Total≈ ₹82
The price has to move about 0.08% in your favour just to break even.

Buy 1 lot at ₹100, sell at ₹110 (65 units)Approximate charges
Gross profit (₹10 × 65)₹650
Brokerage (₹20 × 2)₹40.00
STT (0.15% of ₹7,150 sell premium)≈ ₹10.73

On small option trades, the flat ₹20 brokerage per order is the biggest cost.

How to keep charges under control

  • Trade less. Every round trip costs at least the brokerage on two orders.
  • Avoid tiny trades. Fixed per-order brokerage hits small positions hardest.
  • Read your contract note after each trading day to see the real cost.
  • Include charges when you practise, so your paper results reflect reality.

Common questions

What is the STT on options in 2026?

From 1 April 2026, STT on the sale of options is 0.15% of the premium, and 0.15% of intrinsic value on exercise. Futures STT is 0.05% on the sell side.

Do I pay STT on intraday trades?

Yes. For intraday equity trades, STT is 0.025% on the sell side. Delivery trades pay 0.1% on both buy and sell.

Is GST charged on the full trade value?

No. GST at 18% applies only to brokerage, exchange transaction charges and the SEBI fee — not to the value of the shares or premium.
