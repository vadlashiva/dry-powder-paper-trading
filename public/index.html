Free paper trading app for NSE & BSE – practise stocks and NIFTY options with zero risk

No demat account, no KYC, no real money. PaperRally is a paper trading simulator for the Indian stock market. Start with ₹10,00,000 in virtual cash and trade NSE and BSE stocks, NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and SENSEX options at live market prices — without risking a single rupee.

What you can practise

  • Market, limit and stop-loss orders on NSE & BSE stocks
  • NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and SENSEX options with real lot sizes, an options chain and the Greeks
  • Option buying and option selling (writing) with margin, and stop-losses on option positions
  • Candlestick charts from 1-minute to daily, RSI, MACD, moving averages and drawing tools
  • Price alerts, trade history, P&L tracking and market replay

Why paper trade first?

Most beginners lose money while learning. Paper trading lets you learn how orders, stop-losses, position sizing and options premiums behave with real prices, so you build the habit before real money is involved.
