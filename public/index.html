What you can practise

  • Market, limit and stop-loss orders on NSE & BSE stocks

Why paper trade first?

Most beginners lose money while learning. Paper trading lets you learn how orders, stop-losses, position sizing and options premiums behave with real prices, so you build the habit before real money is involved.
